## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a scholarly, focused, and deeply trusted environment tailored for rigorous research, synthesis, and deep intellectual work. It draws inspiration from a quiet university archive, architectural draft tables, and modern academic journals. The visual narrative combines the tactile poise of ink on archival paper with the structural discipline of modern digital tooling.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile & Editorial** sensibilities:
- **Atmosphere**: Calm, quiet, and grounded. The UI disappears beneath research artifacts, surfacing only to clarify relationships between sources, citations, and syntheses.
- **Audience**: Academics, policy researchers, data analysts, and investigative thinkers who require uncompromised legibility, rapid citation traceability, and zero cognitive clutter.
- **Guiding Principles**:
  - *Trust through restraint*: Minimal chromatic competition. Deep charcoal inks and rich terracotta accents command intent without screaming for attention.
  - *Architectural clarity*: Visible structural borders, predictable rhythm, and strict typographic hierarchy govern layout over gratuitous layers and heavy drop shadows.
  - *Deliberate metadata*: Citations, tokens, timestamps, and model states receive first-class monospaced presentation, signaling precision and auditability.

## Colors

The color palette centers on warm archival neutrals, deep literary inks, and an earthy terracotta anchor.

- **Primary (`#b85c3d` Terracotta)**: Used sparingly for high-intent actions, active citations, primary buttons, and selected states. It evokes terracotta ceramics, warm bookbindings, and scholarly annotations.
- **Secondary (`#1a1a2e` Ink)**: An inky midnight indigo serving as the primary text, structural headers, and highest-contrast baseline.
- **Tertiary (`#0d9488` Teal)**: Reserved for verified citations, provenance seals, operational green lights, and positive status states.
- **Neutral (`#fafafa` Paper)**: Archival off-white background establishing a non-fatiguing, natural reading surface that avoids the sterile glare of pure `#ffffff`.

### State & Theming Rules
- **Light Theme**: Default. `paper` (`#fafafa`) forms the base canvas, while `paper-elevated` (`#ffffff`) surfaces structural cards and modals.
- **Dark Theme Tokens**: When switching modes, surfaces invert to rich charcoal tiers (`#141418` base canvas, `#1c1c24` elevated surfaces), ink transitions to soft bone (`#e8e8e8`), and terracotta transitions to a lifted shade (`#c47d5a`) to maintain optical balance and contrast compliance.
- **Tinted Tonal Fills**: Interactive rows, chip states, and feedback banners leverage 10–15% chromatic tints (`accent-terracotta-subtle`, `success-teal-subtle`, `warning-amber-subtle`, `error-crimson-subtle`) paired with full-strength text and border accents.

## Typography

The typographical structure utilizes a disciplined three-tier stack:
1. **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Display)**: Provides architectural, slightly technical poise with expressive geometry. Tracking is tightened as scale ascends to maintain solid typographic mass.
2. **Inter (Body & Controls)**: Optimized for sustained long-form reading, dense tables, and input forms. Open letterforms and neutral geometry maintain clarity at 12–16px.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Citations, Metadata & Code)**: Explicitly dedicated to citation tags (e.g., `[Ref 4]`), token throughput, timestamps, and model telemetry.

### Typographic Principles
- Headings larger than 30px must transition to `headline-lg-mobile` (24px) below the `sm` breakpoint (`640px`) to prevent awkward word breaks and horizontal overflow.
- All uppercase labels and compact badges (`label-xs`) require slightly expanded tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.03em`) for optical stability.
- Line heights in reading contexts (`body-md`, `body-lg`) are generously spaced at 1.6× to alleviate eye strain during intensive study sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural grid with a 4pt sub-grid for compact controls and fine alignments.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column layout conforming to a maximum content width of `72rem` (1152px) or spanning fluidly with an active workspace drawer. Margin is fixed at `2rem`, gutters at `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Collapses navigation sidebars into an icon rail (64px width).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` safe-margin padding and full-width card reflow. Sidebars retract to an off-canvas drawer.

### Spacing Tokens
- `space-xs` (4px): Fine badge internal margins, icon-to-label gaps.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form label padding, compact list gaps, input vertical padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard component internal padding, horizontal button padding, default vertical stack gap.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card container padding, modal header spacing, distinct section gaps.
- `space-xl` (32px): Primary section dividers, hero margins, research canvas grouping margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a **whisper-soft elevation model** guided by tonal separation and low-contrast perimeter lines rather than prominent drop shadows. 

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Canvas (`paper` `#fafafa`)**: The foundation. Holds zero elevation and no shadows.
- **Resting Containers (`paper-elevated` `#ffffff`)**: Elevated purely through a clean `1px solid #e5e5e5` border and an ultra-subtle diffuse contact shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Hover**: Rather than lifting vertically via dramatic Y-axis shifts, containers display a micro-depth adjustment: border tightens to `border-emphasis` (`#d0d0d0`) and shadow steps to `0 4px 12px rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.08)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Modals, Context Menus, Tooltips)**: 
  - Overlays employ `0 8px 24px rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.12)`.
  - Backdrop dimming uses `rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.4)` to focus user attention entirely on the active task.
  - Tooltips use a crisp, inverted ink surface (`ink` `#1a1a2e` with `ink-inverse` `#ffffff` text) with zero lateral blur: `0 2px 4px rgba(26, 26, 46, 0.1)`.
- **Active / Focused Indicators**: Focus states never rely solely on shadows; they utilize an assertive `2px solid #b85c3d` border reinforced with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 2px rgba(184, 92, 61, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes clear distinction between bounded containers and compact interactive controls:

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Styled with `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px). This creates an approachable, book-like boundary for research notes, citation previews, and query panels without feeling overly casual or bubble-like.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Menu Items**: Styled with `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px). A slightly sharper, deliberate corner radius communicates mechanical precision and deliberate agency.
- **Pills, Badges & Citation Tags**: Badges, status markers, and source reference tags use `rounded-full` (9999px) or `rounded-sm` (0.25rem / 4px) to remain compact and visually distinct from actionable surface cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta (`#b85c3d`) fill with white (`#ffffff`) text. Border radius `rounded-md`. Height 36px (compact) or 40px (default), minimum 44px hit-target container. Hover transitions to `#9d4d33`. Active/pressed scales down to `0.98` with background `#85402b`.
- **Secondary / Subdued**: `accent-terracotta-subtle` (`#fdf2ef`) background with Terracotta (`#b85c3d`) text and `1px solid transparent`. On hover, applies `1px solid #b85c3d`.
- **Ghost / Outline**: Transparent fill, `1px solid #e5e5e5` border, `ink` text. On hover, background shifts to `paper-hover` (`#f5f5f5`) and border to `border-emphasis` (`#d0d0d0`).

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed on `paper-elevated` (`#ffffff`) with `1px solid border-subtle` and `rounded-lg`.
- Padding conforms strictly to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop viewports and `space-md` (16px) on mobile.
- Selected or active reference cards receive a distinctive `2px solid accent-terracotta` left accent border with a subtle warm background tint.

### Citation Chips & Badges
- **Citation Badges**: Styled with `JetBrains Mono`, `0.75rem` (12px), `rounded-full`. Background `paper-hover` (`#f5f5f5`) bordered with `border-subtle`. On hover or citation flash, illuminates with `accent-terracotta-subtle` and terracotta text.
- **Status Badges**:
  - *Ready / Verified*: `success-teal-subtle` container, `success-teal` bold label.
  - *Queued / Processing*: `info-slate-subtle` container, `info-slate` label.
  - *Error / Flagged*: `error-crimson-subtle` container, `error-crimson` label.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Height 40px, `rounded-md`, background `paper-elevated`. Border: `1px solid border-subtle`.
- Placeholder text: `ink-subtle` (`#8a8a9a`). Active text: `ink` (`#1a1a2e`).
- Focus state: Border changes to `#b85c3d` with an external halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(184, 92, 61, 0.25)`. Outline is disabled in favor of this tokenized ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 16px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with `1px solid border-emphasis`.
- Checked state: Filled with `accent-terracotta` with white checkmark/dot indicator.
- Hit target maintains strict 44×44px dimensions via transparent touch boundaries.

### Lists & Source Trees
- Dividers use horizontal `1px solid border-subtle`. Vertical dividers are forbidden inside content lists to avoid visual noise.
- List items feature interactive hover states using `paper-hover` with an immediate `100ms ease-out` transition.
- Selected row displays a tinted `accent-terracotta-subtle` fill with an accent-weighted label.
